Love cannot be expressed by words,
Love neither can be expresso,
Love is expressed by feelings,
that you & yours feel,
for yesterday, tomorrow, and forever,



Love is not within yourself,

but its beneath you;

Love is the most beautiful thing expressed,

May turn a bad into good,

That's how love is.



Love is not always shown,
